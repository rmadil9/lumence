### The three jobs

Chosen by Adil from a list of five. The two left out — refining writing with the LLM, and
reviewing where the day went — are **deliberate, occasional acts**. These three happen *inside*
the work, which is why they decide the design.

**1. Write the day's plan, each morning**
- Must be as fast as typing into a document that is already open.
- Today: alt-tab, type. **Zero aim.**

**2. Capture a thought mid-work, without breaking what he was doing**
- The highest-frequency job in the product.
- Interruption is the failure mode. If capturing a thought costs attention, the thought is lost
  and the tool is blamed.

**3. Commit to a focus block, and later check whether the commitment held**
- The only job that connects stated intention to measured reality
  ([ADR 0004](adr/0004-drop-per-todo-time-tracking.md) left it as the only one).
- The "later check" half is what makes the activity tracker the spine of the product rather than
  an accessory.

### The constraint all three share

**Everything Lumence replaces currently costs nothing to reach.** An open document takes zero
actions. Lumence starts behind on exactly the surfaces that matter most.

The measurable form of this: **how many actions between having the thought and typing?** Today it
is zero or one. Lumence must be one, at most — which makes "where is the cursor when the page
loads" a design decision rather than a detail.

### What Phase 0 rules out

- Any design that serves an imagined second user at Adil's expense.
- Any flow for jobs 1–3 that costs more actions than the tool it replaces.
- Anything that makes capturing a thought require aiming first.
